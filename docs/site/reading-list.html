Reading list — what existing docs, books and papers add to this tutorial

Surveyed on 2026-11-08: the official ROS 2 Jazzy tutorials, MoveIt 2 tutorials, ros2_control docs, the Universal Robots ROS 2 tutorials, two current ROS 2 books, Zivid's (de)palletization guide, and the recent research on robot bin packing and parcel detection. Verdict: the chapter structure holds; seven things were worth borrowing, and they are linked from the chapters they belong to ("Go deeper" sections).

Worth borrowing

Resource What it adds Lands in
UR ROS 2 tutorials: Custom workcell The vendor's own version of Ch 3–6: a workcell description package, the driver started with that description (mock or real), and a MoveIt config on top. Same three-package split as ours, so it is the shortest path from this tutorial to a real UR. Ch 3, 5, 6, 18
MoveIt 2 examples index: Pick and Place with MoveIt Task Constructor, Planning Scene ROS API, Perception Pipeline, Hand-Eye Calibration MTC is MoveIt's framework for exactly the stage sequence pick_place_server hand-rolls; the perception pipeline builds an octomap from the camera so the planner avoids the whole stack; hand-eye calibration is the step every real camera needs. Ch 11, 13, 18
ros2_control: Writing a hardware component and demos example 7 (a 6-axis arm with its own hardware plugin and controller) What replaces mock_components/GenericSystem when you talk to a controller yourself: on_init, read(), write(). Ch 5, 18
Zivid: Depalletization and Palletization Tutorial A camera vendor's field guide: which object features a camera must see, camera placement for stationary vs on-arm mounting, 2D + 3D capture strategy, settings against a time budget, capturing while the robot moves to cut cycle time, production preparation (warm-up, in-field verification, hand-eye, ROI filtering) and maintenance. Ch 9, 13, 18
RoboBPP benchmark (arXiv 2512.04415, 2026) An open benchmark for robotic online bin packing with a physics simulator, three real industrial box datasets and a leaderboard: realistic box sequences for Ch 15 and a physics check for Ch 17. Ch 15, 17
Wang & Hauser, ICRA 2019 and Gao et al., 2025 The heightmap-minimization heuristic that Ch 15 simplifies, with stability and robot-packability constraints; a fast stability validation for online packing. Ch 15
Monica, Aleotti, Lodi Rizzini, IRC 2020 Box detection for tightly packed parcels from a ToF camera using edges from the intensity image plus depth discontinuities: what to do when the gaps Ch 10 relies on are not there. Ch 10

General companions

Resource Use it for
ROS 2 Jazzy tutorials, Intermediate and launch for large projects The official versions of Ch 1, 2, 4 and 16: tf2 broadcasters and listeners, launch substitutions and event handlers, launch_testing, recording and reading bags from a node.
Mastering ROS 2 for Robotics Programming, 4th ed. (Packt, July 2025) Jazzy-based book with chapters on ros2_control, BehaviorTree.CPP, MoveIt 2, the perception stack and CI; the natural next book after this tutorial.
A Concise Introduction to Robot Programming with ROS 2 (CRC Press, 2025) C++-first fundamentals, a complete simulated robot, testing strategies and behavior trees; the same language choice as this tutorial.
Mike Likes Robots: MoveIt Task Constructor walkthrough and Automatic Addison: pick and place with MTC and perception (Jazzy) Two readable, working MTC examples, one with PCL-based perception, if you take the Ch 11 optional port.
Zivid: robotic (de)palletization overview What production cells look like, including a Mujin depalletizing demo with a Zivid camera.

Not borrowed

MoveIt Pro, Isaac Sim and learned (CNN) box segmentation were left out: they are either commercial, GPU-bound in a VM, or need training data you do not have. The structure here lets you add any of them later behind the same /detections and PickPlace interfaces.
